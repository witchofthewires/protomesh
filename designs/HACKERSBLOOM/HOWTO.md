# HACKERSBLOOM Build Guide
![Photograph of a disassembled HACKERSBLOOM solar node (solar panel not pictured)](/static/HACKERSBLOOM/hackersbloom_internal_nosolar_small.jpg "Inside the HACKERSBLOOM")

## The following is not complete!

Quick assembly instructions:
1. Obtain a Lowe's Hampton Bay 1W Solar Light
2. Open it up and snip the cables
3. Unscrew the circuit board
4. Clip the circuit board between the switch and tiny black integrated circuit
5. Screw the circuit board back in
6. Desolder the wires from the solar panel and replace them with a JST cable (triple check polarity!)
7. DO THE REST OF IT
